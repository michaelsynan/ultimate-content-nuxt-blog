# Riverside Pizza Kitchen

Scranton's favorite pizza since 1985.

## Our Story

For over 40 years, Riverside Pizza Kitchen has been serving the Scranton community with authentic, hand-tossed pizza made from our family recipe. What started as a small corner shop has grown into a local institution, but we've never forgotten our roots.

## Menu Highlights

- **New York-Style Pizza** - Hand-tossed dough, signature sauce, premium toppings
- **Specialty Pies** - White pizza, Margherita, Meat Lovers, and more
- **Calzones & Stromboli** - Made to order and baked to perfection
- **Italian Entrees** - Pasta, subs, and classic Italian dinners
- **Wings & Appetizers** - Perfect for game day
