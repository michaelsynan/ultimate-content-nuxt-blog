# Chapter & Verse Books

Your local independent bookstore in the heart of Scranton.

## Our Story

Founded in 2010, Chapter & Verse Books has become a beloved community gathering place for readers of all ages. We carefully curate our selection to feature both bestsellers and hidden gems from independent publishers.

## Services

- **Curated Book Selection** - Fiction, non-fiction, and children's books
- **Special Orders** - We can source almost any title
- **Author Events** - Monthly readings and signings
- **Book Clubs** - Join our community of readers
- **Gift Wrapping** - Complimentary service for all purchases

## Community Hub

More than just a bookstore, we're a space for literary community. Join us for our weekly story time, book clubs, and author events.
