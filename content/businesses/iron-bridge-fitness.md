# Iron Bridge Fitness

Transform your fitness journey at Scranton's premier training facility.

## Our Facility

Spanning 15,000 square feet, Iron Bridge Fitness offers everything you need to achieve your health and wellness goals. From cutting-edge cardio equipment to a dedicated strength training area, we've got you covered.

## What We Offer

- **Personal Training** - One-on-one sessions with certified trainers
- **Group Classes** - HIIT, yoga, spin, and more
- **CrossFit Box** - Dedicated area for functional fitness
- **Nutrition Coaching** - Meal planning and dietary guidance
- **Locker Rooms** - Modern facilities with showers and steam room

## Membership Options

We offer flexible membership plans to fit your lifestyle, from month-to-month to annual commitments. First week is always free for new members!

## Community Focused

Join our supportive community of members who motivate and inspire each other. Regular challenges, events, and workshops keep fitness fun and engaging.
