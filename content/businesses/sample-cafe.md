# Downtown Coffee House

Welcome to Downtown Coffee House, your neighborhood spot for premium coffee and community.

## About Us

Since 2015, we've been roasting our own beans and serving the Scranton community with care. Our baristas are passionate about crafting the perfect cup, whether you prefer a classic espresso or a seasonal specialty drink.

## What We Offer

- **Artisan Coffee** - Single-origin beans roasted in-house
- **Fresh Pastries** - Baked daily by local bakeries
- **Light Lunch** - Sandwiches, salads, and soups
- **Free WiFi** - Perfect for remote work or studying

## Visit Us

Stop by and experience the warmth of local coffee culture!
