# Maple Street Bakery

Freshly baked goodness every morning.

## Our Craft

At Maple Street Bakery, we start baking at 4am every day to ensure you get the freshest breads and pastries possible. Our bakers use time-honored techniques and source ingredients from local farms whenever possible.

## What We Bake

- **Artisan Breads** - Sourdough, baguettes, ciabatta, multigrain
- **Pastries** - Croissants, danishes, scones, muffins
- **Cookies & Brownies** - Classic favorites made fresh daily
- **Custom Cakes** - Birthday, wedding, and special occasion cakes
- **Seasonal Specials** - Holiday treats and limited-time offerings

## Weekly Specials

- **Wednesday** - Fresh sourdough day
- **Friday** - Cinnamon rolls and donuts
- **Saturday** - Farmer's market special breads
- **Sunday** - Fresh bagels and schmear

## Custom Orders

Planning something special? We create custom cakes and dessert platters for any occasion. Order at least 48 hours in advance.
