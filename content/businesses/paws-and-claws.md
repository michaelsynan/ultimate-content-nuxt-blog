# Paws & Claws Pet Grooming

Pamper your furry friend with professional grooming care.

## Our Approach

We treat every pet like they're our own. Our experienced groomers use gentle handling techniques and premium products to ensure your pet looks and feels their best.

## Grooming Services

- **Full Grooming** - Bath, haircut, nail trim, ear cleaning
- **Bath & Brush** - For pets who just need a refresh
- **Nail Trim** - Quick service, no appointment needed
- **Dental Care** - Teeth brushing and breath treatment
- **De-shedding Treatment** - Special process to reduce shedding
- **Specialty Services** - Flea baths, skunk odor removal

## Stress-Free Environment

We maintain a calm, quiet salon to minimize stress for nervous pets. One-on-one attention ensures your pet gets the care they deserve.

## Breed Expertise

From tiny Chihuahuas to giant Great Danes, we groom all breeds and sizes. Ask about our breed-specific cuts.
